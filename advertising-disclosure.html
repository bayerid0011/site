Advertising Disclosure

Last reviewed: October 2026.

Paid distribution

FinGuard Insights may use native, search, social or other advertising to distribute educational articles and tools. Paid distribution does not change the editorial standard, and ad headlines should accurately represent the destination.

No cloaking

The publication does not support cloaking, non-standard redirects designed to show a reviewer different content, or material switching between the reviewed page and the page shown to visitors. No site design can guarantee approval by an advertising network.

Referral relationships

If the operator receives compensation for referring an enquiry to an independent professional provider, that relationship should be disclosed clearly. A referral does not imply a guaranteed outcome.

Advertising data

Optional pixels should follow applicable consent requirements and should not receive sensitive case information, quiz answers, loss values, wallet addresses, emails or phone numbers in event metadata.
