Publication policy

Source-Led Reporting on Financial Safety

FinGuard Insights is an independent educational publication covering online trading, digital assets, payment networks and consumer financial-safety topics. Our purpose is to explain mechanisms, identify evidence and verify claims through primary sources rather than promise a financial outcome.

What we publish

Our six research desks publish long-form explainers on Forex and CFD execution, crypto smart-contract risk, exchange and P2P fraud patterns, binary-options products, SEPA/SWIFT payments and card-network dispute frameworks. We also maintain a dated regulator-warning snapshot and an interactive educational checklist.

How we work

We prefer official regulator material, public payment-network rules, scheme rulebooks, company documents and verifiable transaction records. We distinguish between a regulatory warning, a consumer allegation and a proven legal finding, and between law currently in force and proposals not yet in force.

What we are not

FinGuard Insights is not a regulator, bank, card network, court, law-enforcement agency, crypto exchange or law firm. Any later professional referral should be disclosed separately with its own scope, fees and terms.
