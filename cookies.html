Essential technologies

Essential cookies or local-storage entries may remember privacy choices, theme preferences and basic navigation state and support website security.

Optional analytics and advertising

If Google Analytics, Taboola Pixel or another optional measurement tool is configured, it should load only after the consent required in the visitor's jurisdiction. Campaign measurement should use the minimum data necessary. Quiz answers, financial-loss values, case narratives, wallet addresses, emails and phone numbers should not be included in advertising-event metadata.

Managing preferences

Visitors can choose essential-only operation where required. Before launch, this page should list the exact technologies, providers, purposes and retention periods and provide a persistent preference-management mechanism where required.

Third-party content

Editorial images and primary-source links may connect a browser to third-party services. Production deployment should review those dependencies and self-host assets where appropriate.
