Publication policy

Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 12 October 2026.

FinGuard Insights is an editorial publication about financial safety, regulatory warnings, digital assets and payment systems. This page describes the current GitHub Pages deployment. The weekly digest is not connected to an automated mailing-list provider.

Information you choose to provide

The weekly-digest form asks for your name and email address. Submitting it opens a prefilled email request to compliance@finguard-insights.com; the site does not automatically add addresses to a mailing list.

Article pages also offer an educational transaction-and-platform assessment. It asks for your first name, email address, phone number, country of residence, selected answers about the service, current issue, payment method and timing, plus the consent checkbox. Campaign parameters such as UTM source, campaign, ad set, creative and sub-ID may be included to understand which page or campaign led to the request. Do not enter account credentials, passwords, one-time codes, seed phrases or private keys.

The assessment attempts to submit the request to a server endpoint. The current GitHub Pages host does not execute PHP, so automated delivery is not active on this deployment; when that endpoint is unavailable, the page opens a prefilled email request for you to review and send. Your request is not stored in an automatic mailing list or CRM by this static website. If a form processor or server backend is connected later, this policy must be updated to identify it and explain its retention, security and deletion practices.

Technical information

Hosting and security systems may process standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, referral parameters and server logs to deliver the site, prevent abuse and diagnose errors. The website does not need passwords, one-time codes, seed phrases, private keys or remote-access credentials.

Newsletter and consent

Your digest request is voluntary. The article assessment is also voluntary and is intended to organise basic facts, not to determine legal eligibility or guarantee a result. If you choose to send the generated email, the details you include will be processed by your email provider and received at the editorial contact address. The site currently has no automated unsubscribe system because no mailing-list provider is connected.

Analytics and advertising

Optional analytics or advertising technologies should only be activated in accordance with applicable consent requirements. Personal data and sensitive financial details should not be placed in advertising-event parameters. External regulator websites and other linked services operate under their own privacy notices.

Sharing and international transfers

The publication should not share subscriber data with third parties unless the recipient, purpose and applicable legal basis are disclosed. Before launch, the operator must document its hosting provider, email provider, processors, retention periods and any international data transfers.

Your rights and contact

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Requests can be sent to compliance@finguard-insights.com. The responsible legal entity and a verified business address must be added before public launch.
