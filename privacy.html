Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: October 2026.

FinGuard Insights may process information you submit directly, including name, email address, telephone number, country and answers to the educational Platform Risk Check. Standard technical information may also be processed for security and operation, including IP address, browser information, timestamps, referral parameters and security logs.

Purposes

Information may be used to provide requested educational material, respond to an enquiry, prevent abuse, maintain site security, preserve preferences and measure general site performance where permitted. The website does not require passwords, one-time codes, seed phrases, private keys or remote-access credentials.

Analytics and advertising

Optional analytics or advertising technologies should only be activated in accordance with applicable consent requirements. Sensitive case facts, quiz answers, loss values, wallet addresses, email addresses and phone numbers should not be placed in advertising-event parameters.

Sharing and referrals

If an enquiry is referred to an independent professional provider, the production policy must accurately identify or describe the recipient, purpose and legal basis and obtain any required consent. The real operator, hosting provider, CRM, processors, retention periods and international transfers must be documented before launch.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection. Requests can be sent to compliance@finguard-insights.com.
